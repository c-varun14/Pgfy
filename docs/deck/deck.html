Why I built it

01 · The bill

“Hosting is on you.”

A client wouldn’t pay for infrastructure. One managed Postgres was $20 a month — $240 a year — on top of AI and VPS bills, for an app with a tiny audience. Multiply by every small project an agency runs.

02 · What I tried
DokployOne-click self-hosting, but its databases weren’t managed the way I needed.
RDSDocuments many databases per instance — then leaves every role, password and backup to you.
NeonWorkers every 10 s keep it awake — $19.34 per project, always on.0.25 CU × 730 h × $0.106
03 · What I wanted

One server I own. A database per project in one click. Backups that prove they restore.

In the AI era we all ship niche apps for small audiences: many small databases, none of them big. That is what Pgfy is for.
